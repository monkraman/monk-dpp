# Executive Summary

Monkspaces can strategically build a reusable **Digital Product Passport (DPP) platform** instead of crafting one-off solutions per client.  The EU now mandates DPPs for products (batteries from Feb 2027, iron/steel, textiles, etc. by 2027–2029) under the Ecodesign Regulation (ESPR 2024/1781).  A DPP is a structured digital record of product data – identity, composition, origin, ESG/carbon data, compliance certificates, lifecycle and recycling info – accessible via a data carrier (e.g. QR code).  Its core aims are transparency, sustainability and compliance.  The EU has launched a **DPP Registry** (July 2026) to record unique product IDs and issued harmonised standards for DPP interoperability (CEN-CLC standards EN 18216–18246).   

**Market:** Major DPP platforms target enterprise clients with traceability and compliance needs. Minespider, Circularise and Kezzler (Avery Dennison) lead the space, offering DPPs (and Battery Passports), chain-of-custody, supplier collaboration, APIs, AI extraction and (in some cases) blockchain anchoring.  SME-focused DPP tools also exist (with simpler features and fixed subscriptions).  There is no fixed “off-the-shelf” pricing; enterprise solutions are mostly quote-based. **Approach:** We recommend building an Angular/NestJS/Postgres/S3/Docker solution (or similar modern web stack) in-house, aligned with EU standards.  The core MVP should cover product identity, DPP templates, QR codes, documents, permissions and basic traceability (see Implementation Plan).  Blockchain anchoring and advanced compliance (GS1/EPCIS integration, etc.) can be added later.  Hosting can start on a single VPS (e.g. Hostinger) for ~₹5–8K/month, then scale to multi‑server/AWS for production.  With in-house development, external dev costs are minimal; key costs are cloud (VPS/S3), domain/email, AI/API services, security testing, and the value of developer time.
